Ground Rules
  • Progress by reps first. Add 1–2 reps per set each week, then add load.
  • Move up a phase only if you finished every session. Missed a week? Repeat it.
  • Soreness lasting over 72 hours, or sharp knee or lower-back pain, means repeat the week and do not add volume.
  • No 1RM testing. Use the Load Guide in the Workout tab. If the bar feels slow or you have fewer than 2 reps left, drop 5%.
  • Day 1: weigh in and log it in the Progress tab. If you are several kg off 143, recalculate calories before dieting hard.
  • Every day: 8–10k steps, 10-minute morning mobility flow, protein and water targets, 7.5–9 hours of sleep.

Sunday · Recovery
Rest + Reset
Reset
  • 30 min easy walk in daylight
  • Full CARs circuit: ankle, hip, thoracic, shoulder, 20 min total
  • Pelvic floor 5 min (Kegel holds, pulses, reverse Kegel), diaphragmatic breathing 10 min, sleep 8+ hours
Daily Mobility Minimum
  • Every day, 10 min: spinal waves ×8, hip CARs ×5 each way each leg, shoulder CARs ×5 each way each arm, ankle CARs ×10 each way, thread the needle ×8 each side, deep squat hold 60s.
  • Pelvic floor, 5 min daily: diaphragmatic breathing, Kegel holds 5s ×10, quick pulses ×20, reverse Kegel ×10. This is the item on your Today checklist.
  • Static stretching: 30–60s per muscle after sessions, never before heavy sets.
  • Longer sessions: Wednesday hips 15 min, Saturday loaded mobility 15 min, Sunday CARs circuit 20 min.
Recovery Rules
  • Sleep 7.5–9 hours, steps 8–10k, protein and water targets hit every day.
  • Sunday is a full rest day. Every 4th week is a deload.
  • 48 hours between hard conditioning and heavy leg work.
  • Cut volume by a third for a week if two or more of these show up: resting heart rate 5+ bpm above normal for 3 days, worse sleep, joint pain, bar speed dropping, or you cannot hold your Zone 2 heart rate.
Weekly Frequency Check
  • Push 2× (Mon, Fri), pull 2× (Mon, Fri), squat pattern 2× (Thu, Fri split squat), hinge 2× (Thu, Fri), core 3× (Mon, Thu, Fri).
  • Zone 2: about 135–160 min a week (Wednesday, Saturday and the finishers). Hard conditioning: 1× (Tuesday).
  • Mobility: daily 10 min plus three longer sessions. Hitting each muscle group twice a week is the minimum that best protects muscle in a deficit.
How the Week Is Built
  • One hard day a week. About 80% of your aerobic work is easy (Seiler, 2010), which builds the engine without wrecking your lifting.
  • Hard cardio and heavy legs sit 48 hours apart. Tuesday conditioning, Thursday lower body.
  • Low-impact machines only. Rower, bike, stair climber, sled and carries. No running, jumps or treadmill sprints at this bodyweight (Wilson et al., 2012: running interferes most with strength).
  • Rotate variations. Strength days: Wk1 A, Wk2 B, Wk3 C, Wk4 deload. Tuesday: never the same format twice in a row.
  • When strength and cardio share a day, lift first.
Progression
  • Double progression: hit the top of the rep range on every set with 2 reps left, then add 2.5 kg (bench, press, rows) or 5 kg (squat, deadlift, hip thrust).
  • Pull-ups: drop the assist weight 5 kg per week, or add 1s to your negatives.
  • Zone 2: add 5 min per week up to 75 min. Intervals: add a round every 2 weeks, up to 8 (formats A, E).
  • Every 4th week is a deload: same sessions at 60% load, 3×5, conditioning cut by a third.
  • Track strength with an RPE 8 triple on squat and bench every 4 weeks instead of testing a 1RM.
Calisthenics Integration Roadmap
Start alongside lifting. Skills build in parallel. Uses the gym pull-up bar and dip station.
Phase 1 · Now → Month 6 · Foundation
  • Negative pull-ups: jump to bar, lower in 5s ×5. Daily.
  • Dead hang: 3×30–60s. Builds grip and shoulder health.
  • Hollow body hold: 3×20–30s. Core prerequisite for everything.
  • L-sit on dip bars: 3×5–10s. Hip flexor + core integration.
  • Push-up variations: diamond, archer, slow-tempo 5s down ×3×10.
  • Scapular pull-ups: 3×10. Teaches shoulder blade control.
Phase 2 · Month 6–12 · Skill Building
  • Unassisted pull-ups: aim for 5 clean reps by month 6.
  • Dip progressions: bodyweight → add weight via belt.
  • Pike push-up → elevated pike push-up (handstand push-up progression).
  • Tuck front lever holds: 3×5s. Back and core integration.
  • Wall handstand: kick up to wall, hold 30–60s. Wrist + shoulder.
  • Ring rows: full horizontal, 3×10. Prerequisite for muscle-up.
Phase 3 · Month 12+ · Performance
  • Muscle-up: kipping first → strict progression.
  • Handstand push-up: wall-supported → freestanding.
  • Front lever: tuck → advanced tuck → straddle.
  • Human flag progression: side lever holds.
  • Planche: tuck planche lean → tuck planche hold.

Your squat and bench strength gives you exceptional foundation — your bodyweight drop from 143→85kg will unlock calisthenics skills faster than starting lean. By month 6 you'll be ~120kg which makes pull-ups, dips and L-sits very achievable.
